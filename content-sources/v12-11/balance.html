Berserker

As the originator of "low health equals more damage" classes, Berserker has received a set of updates to reinforce this playstyle. For starters, mana costs have been removed entirely, now opting for health as its sole resource. Breaker now deals damage based on your missing health while also healing you, Broadside now costs health and is a guaranteed crit, and most notably, Forgone Conclusion now acts as toggle skill. While active, your Auto Attack applies a stacking effect that increases your damage at the cost of draining your health on each attack. This effect can be ended at any time, healing you based on the damage dealt while active, usually resulting in a full heal!

System:

  • Mana regeneration model removed (Berserkers are fueled by their rage and have no need for mana).

  • Now costs 2% of Max HP per stack of Berserk.

03/27/26 - Balance Updates

Our last round of March Class updates is here, focusing on adjustments to the Arcane Shield and Event Horizon auras and Legion Revenant and Yami no Ronin classes.

Arcane Shield and Event Horizon Updates:

The current functionality of these auras negatively impacts what we can do while designing future classes and boss fights. We either have to design around them or, in some cases, add other auras specifically to counteract them. Currently, these auras reduce a flat amount of damage received based on Attack Power or Spell Power BEFORE calculating any secondary stats (such as Outgoing Damage or Damage Resistance), in addition to also being able to reduce any True Damage taken for an extended duration, a source of damage that is intended to not be ignorable in most cases. This update changes the damage reduction effect to occur AFTER all other calculations have been made and ignore any instances of True Damage taken.

Arcane Shield: 

  • Damage reduction calculation: Before stat modifiers > After stat modifiers
  • No longer reduces True Damage.
  • Bosses no longer remove Arcane Shield.
  • Can now be applied alongside the Stygian aura from the Acheron Weapon Enhancement.

Event Horizon: 

  • Damage reduction calculation: Before stat modifiers > After stat modifiers
  • No longer reduces True Damage.
  • Bosses no longer remove Event Horizon.

Class Updates

Legion Revenant

Yami No Ronin

Ichi, Ni, and San could often desync from the duration of the Yami, so the effects have been adjusted to make lining them up easier. Additionally, most skills are now long ranged (Nulgath is quaking in his boots), Flash stacks faster, and Revealed no longer reduces Damage Resistance. Yami no Maku's cooldown has also been reduced and Spinning Dragon is now guaranteed to apply, which should allow for some semi-frequent "parry" opportunities. 

System:

  • Outgoing Damage, Crit Chance, and Hit Chance per Flash Stack: 4% > 8%
  • Flash Max Stacks: 20 > 10
  • Revealed Max Stacks: 20 > 10
  • Revealed no longer reduces Damage Resistance.

Tachi:

  • Ichi duration: 9 seconds > 10 seconds

  • San duration: 5 seconds > 6 seconds

  • Now deals damage based on Attack Power instead of Spell Power.
  • Spinning Dragon is now guaranteed to apply.
  • Yami now refreshes its duration when applied again (the accumulated DoT damage also resets).

Ninja Rework

Ninja's skills have been reworked in order to make it play better as a Dodge class, while also putting more emphasis on the characteristics of a Ninja such as stealth, shuriken, smoke bombs, and assassinations.

System:

  • Stat model changed to Dodge Melee
  • Mana Model changed to Rogue

New Skill - Shuriken:

  • Mana Cost: 15
  • Cooldown: 1.5 seconds
  • Damage: 50%
  • Range: Long
  • Applies Rapid Bleed, a fast ticking DoT for 5 seconds, stacks to 8.

Shadowblade:

  • Mana Cost: 25 > 15
  • Cooldown: 12 seconds > 6 seconds
  • Damage: 50% > 200%, damage type changed to Physical.
  • No longer reduces Hit Chance
  • No longer applies the Shadowblade aura.
  • Now applies Sharpened Edge, increasing your Crit Chance by 10% and Crit Damage by 25% for 10 seconds. If Vanish is active, deals increased damage based on how long it's been active, but consumes Vanish. 

New Skill - Smoke Bomb:

  • Mana Cost: 25
  • Cooldown: 10 seconds
  • Damage: 100% Physical
  • Range: Long 
  • Throw a Smoke Bomb at your target, reducing their Hit Chance by 15%. If Vanish is active, your Smoke Bomb catches your target off guard, reducing their Hit Chance by an additional 35%, but consumes Vanish. Both effects last 6 seconds.

Thin Air:

  • Mana Cost: 30 > -20
  • Cooldown: 30 seconds > 15 seconds
  • No longer applies the Thin Air aura.
  • Now applies Vanish, increasing your Haste and Dodge by 100% for 5 seconds.
  • Now applies Concealed Blade for 5 seconds, causing your attacks to deal bonus damage to targets below 25% of their max HP, with the bonus damage increasing the closer they are to death.

Rank 10 Passive:

  • Added the Legendary passive (Assassin and Ninja Warrior Only)

BeastMaster

Increased damage across the board, adjusted playstyle to be more Dodge focused, added a new mechanic that empowers skill effects after striking a target with the Auto Attack. Healing is less than before, but should be more reliable as it now always crits and is on a lower cooldown. Moved the Haste buff into the passive and added a rank 10 passive.

System:

Signal Arrow (Previously Auto Attack):

  • New effect - Follow Up: Using another skill after damaging a target with Signal Arrow will cause your companion to perform a Follow Up, enhancing the effect of that skill. 

Go for the Eyes:

  • Swapped skill order with Borrowed Wings.
  • Damage: 110% > 125%
  • Follow Up: Applies Eye Gouge, reducing Hit Chance by 25% for 6 seconds and applying a larger DoT.

Borrowed Wings (previously Fetch Potion):

  • Swapped skill order with Go for the Eyes.
  • Cooldown: 12 seconds > 8 seconds
  • Damage type: Magical > Physical Now always crits.
  • Now deals Weapon DPS instead of Weapon Damage.
  • No longer applies Radiance
  • Now applies Borrowed Wings, increasing your Dodge by 20% for 6 seconds.
  • Follow Up: Applies Eye in the Sky, increasing your Dodge by 40% for 6 seconds. 

Misdirection:

  • Misdirection aura now reduces Damage Resistance by 15% instead of Haste.
  • Stun chance: 15% > 30%, stun duration: 4 > 2
  • Follow Up: Applies Distracted, reducing Damage Resistance by 30% for 10 seconds.
  • Follow Up: Guaranteed Stun

Treat:

  • Damage: 0% > 300% Physical, can't miss
  • Cooldown: 24 seconds > 16 seconds
  • Treat no longer increases Haste
  • Treat outgoing damage increase: 30% > 20%

Who’s a Good Beastie?:

  • Haste: 10% > 30%

You Are!:

Pet the Beast (new):

  • Starting at rank 1, upon performing a Follow Up you pet your companion, Uplifting them which increases your outgoing physical damage by 5% for 8 seconds, stacks to 8. At rank 10, increases your Crit Chance by 10%.

5/18/24 - Bug Fixes

This week's balance update is all about bug fixes!

  • Fixed a bug on Exalted Harbinger in which the Soul Pact aura was not being applied for the correct amount of time.
  • Adjusted the Burning Ward aura so that it can no longer reduce HP below 1. If a player died to Burning Ward's reflection, it would prevent them from respawning. Additionally, monsters that died to Burning Ward's reflection would not drop anything.
  • Adjusted the Temporal Insanity aura so that it can no longer reduce HP below 1. If a player died to Temporal Insanity's reflection, it would prevent them from respawning. Additionally, monsters that died to Temporal Insanity's reflection would not drop anything.
  • The Temporal Insanity aura now displays an "IMMUNE" message to targets immune to it. When Temporal Insanity was applied to certain monsters, the aura would apply even though Temporal Insanity had no effect against them. To prevent confusion, an "IMMUNE" message now displays on monsters that are immune to the effect.
  • The Counter Attack aura applied by certain monsters can no longer reduce HP below 1. Players could often be disconnected when dying to a monster's Counter Attack aura, this change should prevent any unwanted disconnects and also add a bit more leeway when attacking into bosses with the aura up.

Finally, the One With the Cycle skill has been changed to increase in damage the more it is used against the same target. For the full list of changes, look no further:

  • XXI - The World duration: 22 seconds > 122 seconds

  • Damage Resistance reduction per summoned Arcana: 4% > 2%
  • End of the World stacks applied per skill use: 2 > 1
  • End of the World now reduces Damage Resistance by 4% per stack.
  • End of the World duration: 5 seconds > 8 seconds
  • Each End of the World stack now contributes less damage to the "The Beginning and The End" skill.
  • The bonus damage is now based on the user's End of the World stacks instead of XXI - The World.

Arcana Revocation:

  • Skill can no longer miss.
  • Increased the duration of all 15 second (Reversed) debuffs (except The Lovers and Temperance) to 22 seconds

  •  The High Priestess (Reversed) now reduces Crit Chance instead of Dodge.
  •  The World (Reversed) now reduces Damage Resistance and Crit Chance by an extra 11%, and Dodge by 11% less.
  • Changed the names of the (Reverse) auras to reflect the debuff they apply.
  • Bonus damage while Judgement is active has been increased, but the bonus damage is only only dealt one time to the primary target. Also, Doot Doot.

One With the Cycle:

  • Damage: 100% > 20%
  • No longer does damage based on how long The Fool has been active.
  • Now applies Sacrifice to the target, increasing the damage they take from each successive hit of One With the Cycle by 150%, up to 22 times.
  • Erasure duration: 5 > 3, DoT damage is now based on the amount of End of the World stacks the user has.
