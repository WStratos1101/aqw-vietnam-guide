ArchFiend (0 AC)
AQWorlds Wiki » Items » Classes » ArchFiend (0 AC)

Location: ArchFiend Merge - Tercessuinotlim
Price: N/A

  • Merge the following:
    • Abyssal Contract x1

Sellback: 0 AC
Weapon Damage: 75%, 2.0 speed
Description: Recommended Enhancement: Lucky. The mighty ArchFiends of the Abyss draw power from the eternal darkness, and wash over their foes in a wave of carnage. Conscripted into service for the Abyssal Army for an eternity, ArchFiends are forever loyal.
Stat Model: Full Hybrid
Special Effects: Archfiends gain mana from all hits landed in combat, and especially on crits. The amount depends on damage relative to *their own* HP total. The lower your HP, the less mana you'll heal.
Skills:

+ Mark of Death
- Mark of Death

Mark of Death
Rank Needed: 0
Mana Cost: 0 Mana
Cooldown: 2 Seconds
Type: Physical
Range: A long range attack that deals damage to 2 hostile targets.
Description: Strike your opponents and curse them with a Mark of Death, increasing the damage they take by 4% for 12 seconds, stacks to 8.

+ Fiendish Strike
- Fiendish Strike

Fiendish Strike
Rank Needed: 1
Mana Cost: 15 Mana
Cooldown: 3 Seconds
Type: Hybrid
Range: A long range attack that deals damage to 2 hostile targets.
Description: Lash out at your opponent, dealing damage while drawing strength from the Abyss. Applies a stack of ArchFiend's Mark to yourself, increasing Crit Chance by 5% for 12 seconds, stacks to 8.

+ Consumed by the Abyss
- Consumed by the Abyss

Consumed by the Abyss
Rank Needed: 2
Mana Cost: 15 Mana
Cooldown: 3 Seconds
Type: Hybrid
Range: A long range attack that deals damage to 2 hostile targets.
Description: Unleash the twisted power of the Abyss onto your opponents. You enter your natural Fiendish state, granting you 25% Haste for 6 seconds while your foes are Consumed by the Abyss, lowering their Dodge by 25% for 6 seconds.

+ Fiend Frenzy
- Fiend Frenzy

Fiend Frenzy
Rank Needed: 3
Mana Cost: 25 Mana
Cooldown: 26 Seconds
Type: Hybrid
Range: An infinite range status skill that applies to 7 friendly targets.
Description: Your abyssal power sends you and your allies into a Fiendish Frenzy, increasing Crit Damage by 120% for 8 seconds. If you have stacks of Archfiend's Mark, also apply Fiend Leech to yourself, a 2 second HoT that increases in power based on the number of Archfiend's Mark stacks.

+ Blood of the Abyss
- Blood of the Abyss

Blood of the Abyss
Rank Needed: 4
Type: Passive Ability
Description: Your newfound abyssal lineage increases your effectiveness on the battlefield, increasing Crit Damage by 30%, but reducing your magic damage by 75%.

+ Eyes of the Abyss
- Eyes of the Abyss

Eyes of the Abyss
Rank Needed: 4
Type: Passive Ability
Description: Your abyssal visage grants you superior reflexes, increasing your hit chance and crit chance by 12%.

+ Abyssal Contract
- Abyssal Contract

Abyssal Contract
Rank Needed: 5
Mana Cost: 25 Mana
Cooldown: 26 Seconds
Type: Hybrid
Range: A long range attack that deals damage to 2 hostile targets.
Description: Bind your opponents to the Abyss. Consumes all stacks of Archfiend's Mark and deals damage based on the amount consumed. Also applies Abyssal Contract for 10 seconds, increasing the physical damage your opponents take by 30%, plus 5% for each stack of Mark of Death they have (up to 70% total).

+ Blood Rage
- Blood Rage

Blood Rage
Rank Needed: 10
Type: Passive Ability
Description: Sensing your foes near defeat, below 10% of their health, you have a 25% chance each time you mark your opponent to inflict a heavy DoT.

Notes:

  • Previously called "Legendary ArchFiend" and before "Legendary ArchFiend Class".
  • Class breakdown Design Notes post.
  • Shoulder rune is Color Custom to Eye Color.
  • Brown areas are Color Custom to Base Color.
  • Green areas are Color Custom to Trim Color.
  • Blue areas are Color Custom to Accessory Color.
  • Rune inscription translates to "M" while long inscription translates to "MILTONPOOL".
  • This class has the same skills as ArchFiend (AC).
