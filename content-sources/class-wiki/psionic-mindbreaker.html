Psionic MindBreaker
AQWorlds Wiki » Items » Classes » Psionic MindBreaker

Location: MindBreaker 12K AC Shop - Book of Lore
Price: 0 AC
Sellback: 0 AC
Weapon Damage: 175%, 3.0 Speed
Description: Recommended enhancement: Wizard. An exceptionally well trained MindBreaker, skilled in the art of psionic manipulation. You bend mental energy within yourself and your opponents to your will.
Stat Model: Offensive Caster
Special Effect: Psionic MindBreakers regenerate mana from the psionic flow around them.
Skills:

+ Psychic Assault
- Psychic Assault

Psychic Assault
Rank Needed: 0
Mana Cost: 0 Mana
Cooldown: 3 Seconds
Type: Physical
Range: A short range attack that deals damage to 2 hostile targets.
Description: Assail two opponents with your mental attacks, clouding their minds and decreasing their crit chance by 25% for 4 seconds.
Note: Applies Clouded Mind! to 2 enemies, decreasing their crit chance by 25% for 4 seconds.

+ Mental Flare
- Mental Flare

Mental Flare
Rank Needed: 1
Mana Cost: 8 Mana
Cooldown: 3 Seconds
Type: Magical
Range: A long range attack that deals damage to 2 hostile targets.
Description: Sharpen your mind and engulf two foes in psionic flames. Always hits and crits. For every target you hit, gain a stack of Concentration, increasing your magical damage dealt by 30%, stacking to 6, and lasting 4 seconds.
Notes:

  • Applies Concentration! to the caster, increasing magical damage by 30% for 4 seconds.
  • Concentration! only stacks to 5, and increases magical damage by 30% at 1 stack, 120% at 2 stacks, 150% at 3 stacks, 180% at 4 stacks and 210% at 5 stacks.
+ Psion Drain
- Psion Drain

Psion Drain
Rank Needed: 2
Mana Cost: 0 Mana
Cooldown: 6 Seconds
Type: Magical
Range: An long range attack that deals damage to yourself.
Description: Open your mind and allow psionic energy to flow in, recovering 35 mana and reducing your mana costs by 10% for 10 seconds, up to 4 stacks. Doing so, however, overloads your senses, causing you to take damage.
Notes:

  • Applies Mana Flow! to the caster, reducing mana costs by for 10 seconds.
  • Mana Flow! only stacks to 3, and reduces mana costs by 5% at 1 stack, 40% at 2 stacks and 50% at 3 stacks.
+ Cerebral Siphon
- Cerebral Siphon

Cerebral Siphon
Rank Needed: 3
Mana Cost: 20 Mana
Cooldown: 6 Seconds
Type: Magical
Range: A long range attack that deals damage to 1 target.
Description: Reach out and invade your opponent’s mind, stealing their life force and healing yourself for half of your missing hp. Your assault leaves them weak, reducing their defense by 40% for 15 seconds. Cannot be avoided, but cannot crit.
Notes:

  • Applies Weak Minded! to 1 enemy, reducing defense by 40% for 15 seconds.
  • Heal is negated by Decay.
