Map Name: tercessuinotlim
Room Limit: 6
Access Points:

  • /join tercessuinotlim
  • Click on Nulgath House Guest (Owner only)
  • Citadel - Through the portal on Screen 7
  • Enchanted Nulgath Nation House - Multiple buttons from Nation House Portal
  • Event Hub - 'Tercessuinotlim' button (After completing the 'Beyond the Portal' quest)

AI:

  • ??? (Monster) - Aggressive on entering Screen 24.
  • Nulgath (Monster) - Aggressive on movement.
  • Shadow of Nulgath (Monster) (1) - Aggressive on movement and on respawn on Screen 22.

Notes:

  • Must have completed the 'Beyond the Portal' quest to access this location.
  • This is a locked zone.
  • Not all pathways are linear, but they are not random.
  • Tercessuinotlim is "MiltoniusSecret" spelled backwards.
  • The following directions start at the first screen:
    • Casparilla/Yin/Yang: Right, left, center, right and right.
    • Swindle/Toxic Fiend Helm: Right, left, center, right, right and right.
    • Jadzia: Right, left, center, right, right, right and right.
    • Nulgath/Skew: Left, center, upper left, down, middle, right, middle, right, right, left and left.
    • Polish/Bamboozle: Right, left and center.
    • Taro/Void HighLord/Zee: Right, left, center and right.
    • Lae: Left, center and upper left.
    • Carnage: Left, center.
  • Clicking on Swindle Bilk in the top right corner of Screen 1 (with Swindle Bilk's To Go Hut in your inventory) takes you to Screen 16 (Swindle Bilk).
  • This location provides a Character Page Background on Screen 21.
