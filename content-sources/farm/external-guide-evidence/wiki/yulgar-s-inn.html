Notes:

  • This location provides a Character Page Background on Screen 1.
  • Clicking on the Daily Quest Board on Screen 6 opens Daily Quest Board's Quests.
  • Clicking on the pink dragon above Cysero's head will open the Bubblebath Toy Shop.
  • Clicking on the Twilly painting on Screen 7 automatically sends you to Shadow Realm if you are at least level 45.
  • Clicking the dartboard on Screen 8 will cause the player to throw a knife.
  • You can punt the penguin on Screen 4 if you walk up to it.
