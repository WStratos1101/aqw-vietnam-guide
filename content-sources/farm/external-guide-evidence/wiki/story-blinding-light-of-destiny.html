Blinding Light of Destiny Questline

Part One: Starting Off

Quest Location: Necropolis
Quests Begun From: Artix (NPC)

  • Reforging the Blinding Light
  • Secret Order of Undead Slayers
  • Essential Essences
  • Bust some Dust
  • A Loyal Follower

Note: This quest can only be completed once.

To reforge the Blinding Light of Destiny and create other weapons infused with pure light… we are first going to need what remains of the original battleaxe. Can you find the Blinding Light of Destiny's Handle? (Obtained from one of the DoomWood Part 1 Endings)

+ When Completed
- When Completed

AWESOME! You found the handle! I really missed this axe. So many memories. Wow… we really blew it up good, huh? Not much of the handle left. Hmmmm.. was Daimyo chewing on this? Daimyo says, "Arf!" Alright… what is next?

Items Required:

  • Blinding Light of Destiny Handle

Rewards:

  • 7,770 Gold
  • 2,000 Exp
  • 500 Rep : DoomWood

Requirements: Must have completed the previous quest.
Note: This quest can only be completed once.

The weapons we are going to forge are for battling the evil undead. So… there is something I have been wanting to ask you for a long time. Would you… like to join the Secret Order of Undead Slayers? Simply present your Bone Grinder Medal (Obtained from Arryd after defeating 100 undead waves in DoomWood Part 1.) and I will make it official!

+ When Completed
- When Completed

Congratulations! You are now an initiate of the Secret Order of Undead Slayers. Remember… the first rule of the Secret Order of Undead Slayers… it is a secret. Top secret! So be sure to tell everyone on the forums and Twitter that they are not allowed to tell anyone else that you are now in the Secret Order of Undead Slayers!

Items Required:

  • Bonegrinder Medal

Rewards:

  • 2,000 Gold
  • 500 Exp
  • 700 Rep : DoomWood

Requirements: Must have completed the previous quest.

Now that you are an initiate of the Secret Order of Undead Slayers, I am going to let you in on a little secret: deep beneath Battleon lives an onslaught of Undead. Some of the Undead harness a strange, mystic soul called "Undead Essence." We must study this! Travel into these dangerous depths and recover 25 Undead Essences. I would like to take a whack at them and see if there is anything hiding within…

- When Completed

What a discovery! Since the Undead are not really alive… but are not dead, either… that means they still have a spirit. We need to do everything we can to free their Spirit Orbs! I bet Bone Dust from the Undeads are full of Spirit Orbs. Try checking the Undead that patrol the Swordhaven Graveyard and Battleunder and collect whatever Bone Dust you find.

Gather 100 Spirit Orbs and a Celestial Compass from an Ephemerite and return them to me so that I can send them to the afterlife. After-after life? I am not sure where Undead Spirit Orbs go…

Part Two: Metal Enchantments

  • Mine Crafting
  • Pious Platinum Enchantment
  • Sanctified Silver Enchantment
  • Celestial Copper Enchantment
  • Immortal Iron Enchantment
  • Glorious Gold Enchantment
  • Blessed Barium Enchantment
  • Almighty Aluminum Enchantment

Requirements: Must have completed the 'A Loyal Follower' quest.
Notes:

  • This quest can only be completed once per day.
  • This quest can also be found from Daily Quest Board in Yulgar's Inn.
  • This quest name is a direct reference to the popular sandbox indie game Minecraft.

Reforging weapons infused with pure light is going to take A LOT of metal. I tried rocking out on the guitar with Zhoom but… that is not the metal we were looking for. We need to harvest metal deposits from Raw Ore. Search all over Lore for beasts affiliated with stones, rocks, crystals, metals, earth, and golems. They should be full of Raw Ore deposits… which is a good thing, because we will need 30 of them! Also, we will need an Axe of the Prospector from a Balboa to mine the metal out of the Raw Ore. Good luck, prospective prospecting paladin!

- When Completed

Requirements: Must have completed the 'Mine Crafting' quest.

Now that we have collected raw metal, we can begin to enchant it with righteous power. But we are going to need the proper materials. Gather 25 Undead Energy from Lore…s skeletal and Undead minions. Additionally, we will also need 5 Spirit Orbs and 25 Paladaffodils for the enchantment to take affect. The Paladaffodils bloom on plant and tree-like monsters all over Lore, like Wisteria, Treeants, and Marsh Trees. When you have found everything, we can combine them with the raw metal and the enchantment will be complete! Good luck!

Note: These quests cannot be accessed after completing one of them.

Things are looking great so far! We have Spirit Orbs and Enchanted Metals… and our next step now is to forge them together! Once forged, the metal will become stronger than ever before, both physically and spiritually. Items of ''Blinding Light’’ can only be created with ‘‘Metals of Destiny,’’ and the only forge hot enough to craft these metals resides in the Dwarfhold Mountains. Tell Geopetal, the Rock Dweller, that I sent you and I am sure she will forge us those Metals of Destiny!
