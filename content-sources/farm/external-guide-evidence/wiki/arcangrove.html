Arcangrove
AQWorlds Wiki » World » Locations » Arcangrove » Arcangrove

Hub Town-Level 25
A beautiful, thriving forest home to many creatures of magical descent. Ancient magi have studied in the notable Tower of Magic, a place where magic users shall learn, evolve, and be tested. The Tower also guards the portal to the Para-Elemental Plane of Magic where few have ever traveled.
