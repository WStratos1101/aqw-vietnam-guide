Mobius
AQWorlds Wiki » World » Locations » Chiral Valley » Mobius

Hub Town-Level 6 to 7
The small village of Mobius has been twisted by Chaos Lord Escherion's inversion magic, turnning everything inside-out, upside-down and backwards!

Mini-Games:

  • Runix Cube

Monsters:

  • Chaos Sp-Eye (Level 8) x8
  • Cyclops Raider x7
  • Fire Imp (Monster) (1) x8
  • Slugfit (Monster) x1

During Za'Nar Void Invasion:

  • Random Spawns x12
    • Chiral Frask
    • Chiral Shade

Map Name: mobius
Room Limit: 6
Access Points:

  • /join mobius
  • Amberheart Watchtower - West of Screen 1
  • Book of Lore
    • 'Mobius' button from the Escherion achievement
    • 'Mobius' button from the Hydrated achievement
  • Button in Greenguard West map
  • Castle Whistler - South of Screen 1
  • Cornelis Ruins - East of Screen 3 or talk to Anise
  • Cysero's Clubhouse - Portal on Screen 1
  • Escherion's Tower - 'To Mobius' button from Magic Lamp, Renn or Zio after completing the 'Escherion' quest
  • Faerie Forest
    • West of Screen 1
    • Talk to Dew Drop in Screen 3
  • Greenguard West - South of Screen 1
  • Hydra Challenge - South of Screen 1
  • Hydra Lake - North of Screen 4
  • Museum - Northeast at the CrossRoads
  • Oasis - South of Screen 3
  • Otto - North
  • Relativity - The teleporter pad on Screen 4
  • Shadowfall - 'To Chiral Valley' button from Gravelyn (NPC)
  • Story (Location) - North of Screen 3
  • Chiral Valley Map

Note: This location is a parody of the M. C. Escher painting 'Relativity'.
