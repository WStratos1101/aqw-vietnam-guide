Lair
Galanoth has fallen to the zombie fog! The DragonSlayer is not trying to slay the dragons… he is trying to BITE them! I never thought this would happen… but I need to DEFEND the dragons from Galanoth! AND slay the dragons that have already been zombified!

Mythsong
Warlic has fallen to the zombie fog! I do not know what affect this will have on the ArchMage’s unusual magical abilities, but I must prevent him from using them to help the undead horde!

Arcangrove
Both Alina and Cysero have fallen to the zombie fog! Our hope for a cure is gone, and so are the PotionMistress and Mad Weaponsmith. They must not be allowed to use their knowledge to aid the undead. I will do what I must… forgive me, my friends!

Willowshire
Zhoom has fallen to the zombie fog! If he retains his dexterity and accuracy with the bow, he could take out innocent villagers before they even know he is there. I will have to keep the Ranger from roaming…and prevent him from entering Battleon!

Battleon
This is the final battle. All of Lore is in the balance. I must not fail, I must not flee. Artix and all of these people depend on me. I have seen so many of my friends fall to this zombie fog, but if I can help it, they will be the last!
