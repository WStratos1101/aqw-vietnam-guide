Bloodtusk Ravine
AQWorlds Wiki » World » Locations » Bloodtusk » Bloodtusk Ravine

Field-Level 1 to 25
The ravine divides the land and separates the Horc and Troll tribes which live there. For centuries, they have known an uneasy peace, but that time is at an end!
