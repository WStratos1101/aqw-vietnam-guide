Trissa's Quests
AQWorlds Wiki » World » Quests » Trissa's Quests

Quest Location: Museum
Quests Begun From: Trissa
Note: These quests can only be completed once.

  • The Dawn of Lore
  • Mystical Magics
  • Science Rules
  • War: What is it Good For?
  • Which Came First, the Dragon or the Dragonoid?
  • Artifact Hunting
  • What Does Lore Have in Store?
  • Awe-scention

Requirements: Must be Level 25.

We've been looking to expand the museum but first we need some more artifacts to display in it. Your first list contains relics linked to ancient civilizations. If you find anything during your travels, please bring it back to us and there'll be some gold in it for you. We need Fossilized Egg Yolks from a city in a mountain, a Stone Mask, last seen in the possession of a strangely attractive vampire, Ancient Bones from a giant, chaotic skeleton, and an Intact Golem Head from ruins now overrun by Gargoyles.

+ When Completed
- When Completed

I didn't expect you to find all of these, I'm impressed.

Items Required:

  • Fossilized Egg Yolk x12
    • Dropped by:
      • Chaos Egg (Level 1)
      • Chaos Egg (Level 5) (Version 1)
  • Stone Mask x1
    • Dropped by Edvard
  • Ancient Bone x6
    • Dropped by O-Dokuro (Level 23)
  • Block Head

Rewards:

  • 10,000 Gold
  • 25,000 Exp
  • 1,250 Rep : TreasureHunter

Requirements:

  • Must have completed the previous quest.
  • Must be Level 25.

Magic, the lifeblood of Lore. A force so prominent that there are clear signs of its use from ages ago. Our researchers have been investigating the primitive stages of magic but are in need of more components in order to continue. Current research has given us a lead on these components, but we need you to find them for us. We need Enchanted Manuscripts, from the realm of Death, The Rite of Renewal which was stolen by the Grand Inquisitor of the Citadel, The Coven’s Sigil, used by the Witches of the Marsh, and Spell Stones from the ruined city of Gilead.

+ When Completed
- When Completed

Scrolls, Sigils, Manuscripts, all components that were once needed to cast spells. It's amazing how far magic has come. These will make a fine addition to the museum after they've been researched.

Items Required:

  • Enchanted Manuscript x8
    • Dropped by Undead Mage (Level 35)
  • Rite of Renewal x1
    • Dropped by Grand Inquisitor (Level 25)
  • Coven's Sigil x1
    • Dropped by Dark Witch
  • Spell Stone x10
    • Dropped by:
      • Bubblin
      • Earth Elemental (1)
      • Fire Elemental (Monster) (1) (Level 30)
      • Mana Elemental
      • Water Elemental (1) (Level 30) (Version 1)
      • Wind Elemental (3) (Level 30)

Rewards:

  • 10,000 Gold
  • 25,000 Exp
  • 1,250 Rep : TreasureHunter

Requirements:

  • Must have completed the previous quest.
  • Must be Level 25.

While science hasn't existed for as long as magic, its rapid developments have made it just as impressive. Like magic, our researchers have been looking into the early stages of science but have come to a roadblock. We need Supercharged Gems, found in the mountains of Thunderfang, Lightning Capacitors from a Mad Scientist's monster, A Wooden Control Panel from the Sneevil's War Machine, and then we'll need a Core of some kind.

+ When Completed
- When Completed

That core you found, what's it from? A DRAGONOID? Those were said to be a myth! This will be looked into immediately.

Items Required:

  • Supercharged Gem x8
    • Dropped by Energy Elemental (Version 1)
  • Lightning Capacitor x5
    • Dropped by Frank
  • Wooden Control Panel x1
    • Dropped by Sneeviltron (Monster)
  • Dragonoid Core x1
    • Dropped by Dragonoid

Rewards:

  • 10,000 Gold
  • 25,000 Exp
  • 1,250 Rep : TreasureHunter

Requirements:

  • Must have completed the previous quest.
  • Must be Level 25.
  • Quest title is a direct reference to the song War.

While the Dragonoid Core is being researched, I have another task for you. As a part of the museum's expansion, we've been looking to add a section on wars throughout Lore's history. Someone of your caliber must've experienced a fair amount of them already. Would you mind visiting past battlegrounds and bringing back some keepsakes that could be displayed in the museum?

+ When Completed
- When Completed

Thank you, Hero. Wars that are forgotten are doomed to be repeated. Oh, sounds like the researchers are done with the Dragonoid Core, let's see what they've found.

Items Required:

  • O-dokuro's Tooth
  • D'wain Jonsen's Stinger x1
    • Dropped by D'wain Jonsen
  • Music Pirate's Instrument of War x6
    • Dropped by:
      • Flying Music Pirate
      • Music Pirate (Level 27) (Version 1)
  • Noxus' Necromancy Robe x1
    • Dropped by Noxus (Monster)

Rewards:

  • 10,000 Gold
  • 25,000 Exp
  • 1,250 Rep : TreasureHunter

Requirements:

  • Must have completed the previous quest.
  • Must be Level 25.

Our researchers just informed me that the Dragonoid Core you found was built by the dragons themselves! To think that magical creatures once used science, who would've guessed? This has raised a question as to why dragons no longer seem to utilize any forms of technology. The Dwakel are technologically advanced and are believed to be descendents of dragons. Could you bring back some of their technology from /crashsite so we can see if there's any deeper connection?

+ When Completed
- When Completed

After examining the Dwakel technology you brought back, our researchers have formed a hypothesis. Much like how the Dwakel aren't dragons, the dragons that built the Dragonoid weren't dragons either! At least not the same dragons we see here on Lore today.

Items Required:

  • Dwakel Shield Generator x2
    • Dropped by:
      • Barrier Bot
      • Barrier Sentry
  • Dwakel Weapon x10
    • Dropped by:
      • Dwakel Blaster
      • Dwakel Warrior (Monster) (Version 1)
      • Flamethrower Dwakel (Level 15)
  • Mithril Man's Power Armor x1
    • Dropped by Mithril Man
  • Protosartorium's Power Armor x1
    • Dropped by ProtoSartorium (Monster)

Rewards:

  • 10,000 Gold
  • 25,000 Exp
  • 1,250 Rep : TreasureHunter

Requirements:

  • Must have completed the previous quest.
  • Must be Level 50.

Throughout your journey you may come across objects of great power, if you can get a hold of these items, our researchers would love to study them closer. You can get the Blade of Awe from Valencia, the Supreme Arcane Staff of Arcangrove, A Destiny Weapon, or what's left of one, A Doom Weapon, and The Mirror Shield of Cornelis, shattered by the Gargoyles that now inhabit the city.

+ When Completed
- When Completed

The Blade of Awe, is this real? Oh, the other stuff you found is cool too though!

Items Required:

  • The Supreme Arcane Staff
  • Blinding Light of Destiny Handle
  • ShadowReaper Of Doom
  • Blade of Awe
  • Mirror Shield Fragment x50

Rewards:

  • 500,000 Gold
  • 1,000,000 Exp
  • 10,000 Rep : TreasureHunter

Requirements:

  • Must have completed the previous quest.
  • Must be Level 75.

This next list contains weapons and armors that once belonged to powerful combatants. Their whereabouts these days are mostly unknown, but maybe they haven't strayed too far from their birthplace. The helmet of Vaden, DeathKnight Lord of the Castle of Bone, The armor of Zular, Warden of the Djinn Gate, Fortitude and Hubris, the Balanced Blades, the armor of a Shadow Dragon Defender, thought to have been consumed by the Void, an ancient Paladin's armor. My cousin Jill'Ette's been after it for a while now, and Valoth's Cannon of Doom. Talk to the Dwakel Scavenger, wherever they are.

+ When Completed
- When Completed

Your efforts are very much appreciated. Oh, you had to pay for the cannon? We'll reimburse you for that. Buying treasures from collectors is something we do all the time.

Items Required:

  • Vaden's Helm
  • Armor of Zular
  • Fortitude + Hubris
  • Shadow Dragon Defender
  • Silver Exalted Paladin
  • Valoth's Cannon of Doom

Rewards:

  • 7,000,000 Gold
  • 5,000,000 Exp
  • 15,000 Rep : TreasureHunter

Requirements:

  • Must have completed the previous quest.
  • Must be Level 100.

Many of these treasures have been lost to time and will likely require you to undertake perilous tasks in order to retrieve them. If you do manage to find everything, then you'll be rewarded handsomely, I can assure you of that. I need the armor of Drakath, Champion of Chaos, the armor and helmet of Awethur (ask Valencia about these), The helmet of Sepulchure, the armor of an ancient DoomKnight, and the armor of renowned sellsword Bin Jett, unfortunately he was swallowed by a slimy dragon long ago.

+ When Completed
- When Completed

Never thought I'd see the day. Here, this is yours, you've earned it a thousand times over.

Items Required:

  • Original Drakath Armor
  • Armor of Awe (Armor)
  • Helm of Awe
  • Sepulchure's Original Helm
  • Arch DoomKnight
  • Bin Jett's Salvaged Armor Part x50

Rewards:

  • 10,000,000 Gold
  • 0 Exp
  • 30,000 Rep : TreasureHunter
  • Awe Ascension Character Page Badge
