Doom Metal Necro
AQWorlds Wiki » Items » Classes » Doom Metal Necro

Location: Skull Dome
Price: N/A (Reward from the 'Doom Metal Necro Class' quest)
Sellback: 0 AC
Weapon Damage: 35%, 2.0 speed
Base Level: 60
Description: [Duplicate of Heavy Metal Necro Class] Recommended Enhancement: Fighter/Luck Dark Metal Necros are a force to be reckoned with in a mosh pit. Like the band they're die-hard fans of, they utilize their deathly riffs, mighty voices, and necromantic abilities to crush their foes into submission.
Stat Model: Tank Melee
Special Effects:

  • Heavy Metal Necros gain mana when they:
    • Strike an enemy in combat (more effective on crits)
    • Are struck by an enemy in combat

Skills:

+ Twisted Slash
- Twisted Slash

Twisted Slash
Rank Needed: 0
Mana Cost: 0 Mana
Cooldown: 2 Seconds
Type: Physical
Range: A medium range attack that deals damage to 1 target.
Description: Damage your enemies with a cruel cut. Twist their senses, reducing their Haste by 25% for 10 seconds.
Note: Applies Twisted, reducing enemy haste by 25% for 10 seconds.

+ Rotting Riff
- Rotting Riff

Rotting Riff
Rank Needed: 1
Mana Cost: 8 Mana
Cooldown: 5 Seconds
Type: Physical
Range: A medium range attack that deals damage to 1 target.
Description: Unleash a wicked soundwave, summoning otherwordly ghouls that mob your foe. Applies Reverberating, which increases all outgoing damage by 25% for 3 seconds. Also applies Forgone Conclusion, lasting 4 seconds and restoring health based on damage dealt during the duration upon expiry.

+ Shattering Sanity
- Shattering Sanity

Shattering Sanity
Rank Needed: 2
Mana Cost: 15 Mana
Cooldown: 5 Seconds
Type: Physical
Range: A medium range attack that deals damage to 1 target.
Description: Conjure an unholy undead minion to assail your opponent's body and mind, dealing massive damage that increases the lower your health is. The assault leaves your foe with a Shattered Psyche, decreasing their Crit Chance by 20% for 10 seconds. If Cursed Echo is present, you gain Insanity, increasing your Crit Chance by 30% for 40 seconds.

+ Deathly Roar
- Deathly Roar

Deathly Roar
Rank Needed: 3
Mana Cost: 18 Mana
Cooldown: 5 Seconds
Type: Physical
Range: A long range attack that deals damage to 1 target.
Description: Unleash sonic destruction upon your foe, Terrifying them and reducing their dodge by 30% for 14 seconds. You enter a Deranged Frenzy, increasing your haste by 30% for 14 seconds.

- Sacrificial Pact

Sacrificial Pact
Rank Needed: 5
Mana Cost: 0 Mana
Cooldown: 3 Seconds
Type: Physical
Range: A short range attack that deals damage to yourself.
Description: Sacrifice your health and swear upon a Dark Pact, increasing the damage you take by 33% for 5 seconds and stacks up to 3. If Reverberating is present, the pact grants you great power, granting you Cursed Echo and increasing your damage dealt by 33% for 8 seconds, stacking to 3.
