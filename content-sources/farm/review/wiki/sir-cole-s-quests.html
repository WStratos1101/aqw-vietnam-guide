Sir Cole's Quests
AQWorlds Wiki » World » Quests » Sir Cole's Quests

Quest Location: Inn at the Edge of Time
Quests Begun From: Sir Cole

  • Unlocking the Antechamber
  • Ezrajal
  • Unlocking the Reliquary
  • The Warden
  • Unlocking the Apex
  • The Engineer

Note: This quest can only be completed once.

The gates to the Antechamber of the Exaltia Tower are sealed by a special elemental lock. You will need to gather the appropriate Elemental Essences of Fire and Ice to proceed into the Tower.

+ When Completed
- When Completed

The gates to the Antechamber are now open. Face your first opponent in the Arena of Time!

Items Required:

  • Key Fire Essence x3
    • Dropped by Fire Elemental (Monster) (2) (Level 65)
  • Key Ice Essence x3
    • Dropped by Ice Elemental (4)

Rewards:

  • 5,000 Gold
  • 30,000 Exp

Requirements: Must have completed the previous quest.

In the Antechamber waits Ezrajal, an Exalted tasked with protecting the entrance of the Exaltia Tower. You must defeat him if you wish to ascend the Tower and claim your prize at the end!

+ When Completed
- When Completed

You have defeated Ezrajal. Well done! But do you have what it takes to make it to the Apex?

Items Required:

  • Ezrajal Defeated x1
    • Dropped by Ezrajal

Rewards:

  • 3,000 Gold
  • 10,000 Exp

Requirements: Must have completed the previous quest.
Note: This quest can only be completed once.

The next floor of the Tower is the Reliquary. In order to access it, you will have to undo its elemental lock, much like before. This time, the gates are sealed with locks of Nature and Wind. Gather the Essences and proceed to the Tower's Reliquary.

+ When Completed
- When Completed

The Reliquary awaits you, and within it, it's Warden!

Items Required:

  • Key Nature Essence x3
    • Dropped by Nature Elemental
  • Key Wind Essence x3
    • Dropped by Wind Elemental (2) (Level 65)

Rewards:

  • 5,000 Gold
  • 30,000 Exp

Requirements: Must have completed the previous quest.

The Warden guards the Reliquary and all its contents. If you are to progress to the Tower's Apex, it must be dealt with.

+ When Completed
- When Completed

Defeating the Warden is no easy feat. Congratulations and proceed to the Tower's Apex.

Items Required:

  • The Warden Defeated x1
    • Dropped by The Warden

Rewards:

  • 3,000 Gold
  • 10,000 Exp

Requirements: Must have completed the previous quest.
Note: This quest can only be completed once.

The final gate keeps you from the Apex. You must collect the Energy and Water Essences that keep the gate sealed in order to face the Tower's final foe.

+ When Completed
- When Completed

The final gate is open! Proceed to the Apex and face the Tower's creator!

Items Required:

  • Key Energy Essence x3
    • Dropped by Energy Elemental (2)
  • Key Water Essence x3
    • Dropped by Water Elemental (2) (Level 65)

Rewards:

  • 5,000 Gold
  • 30,000 Exp

Requirements: Must have completed the previous quest.

Atop the Tower's Apex stands the Engineer, protecting the fabled weapon used in The Final Stand of Ehr'Ishin. They will not go easily, but neither shall you.

+ When Completed
- When Completed

With the Engineer defeated, you have explored the Exaltia Tower of your reality! Care to do battle with their ultra forms?

Items Required:

  • The Engineer Defeated x1
    • Dropped by The Engineer

Rewards:

  • 3,000 Gold
  • 10,000 Exp

Quest Location: Inn at the Edge of Time
Quests Begun From: Sir Cole
Requirements: Must have completed the 'The Engineer' quest.
Note: These quests can only be completed once per day.

  • Ultra Ezrajal
  • Ultra Warden
  • Ultra Engineer

Note: This quest is also accessible in Ultra Ezrajal (Location).

Before you stands a more challenging version of Ezrajal. Do you have what it takes to defeat him?

+ When Completed
- When Completed

You have conquered the Challenge of Ultra Ezrajal! The Antechamber is your domain now!

Items Required:

  • Ultra Ezrajal Defeated x1
    • Dropped by Ultra Ezrajal (Monster)

Rewards:

  • 250,000 Gold
  • 500,000 Exp

Items:

  • Ezrajal Insignia x1

Requirements: Must have completed the previous quest.
Note: This quest is also accessible in Ultra Warden (Location).

This is the strongest version of the Warden I could find throughout this multiverse's timelines! Can you defeat it?

+ When Completed
- When Completed

You have conquered the Challenge of the Ultra Warden! The Reliquary's treasures are yours to claim!

Items Required:

  • Ultra Warden Defeated x1
    • Dropped by Ultra Warden (Monster)

Rewards:

  • 250,000 Gold
  • 500,000 Exp

Items:

  • Warden Insignia x1

Requirements: Must have completed the previous quest.
Note: This quest is also accessible in Ultra Engineer (Location).

Is this the Engineer's true power, as the one who stands above all others? Defeat them to climb ever-higher!

+ When Completed
- When Completed

You have conquered the Challenge of the Ultra Engineer! You stand above the Exalted themselves!

Items Required:

  • Ultra Engineer Defeated x1
    • Dropped by:
      • Ultra Engineer (Monster)
      • Yoshino's Engineer

Rewards:

  • 250,000 Gold
  • 500,000 Exp

Items:

  • Engineer Insignia x1

Quest Location: Inn at the Edge of Time
Quests Begun From: Sir Cole
Requirements: Must have completed the 'The Engineer' quest.

  • Exalted Apotheosis

Requirements: Must have Exalted Apotheosis in your inventory.
Note: This quest can only be completed once.

Hero, I can't believe you've managed to recreate the legendary weapon of the Exalted! Bring me one Exalted Node, and I will award you with a character page badge.

+ When Completed
- When Completed

Congratulations!

Items Required:

  • Exalted Node x1

Rewards:

  • 0 Gold
  • 0 Exp
  • Apotheosis Character Page Badge
