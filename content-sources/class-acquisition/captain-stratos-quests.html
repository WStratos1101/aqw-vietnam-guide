Hero! An army of Sky Pirate Draconians have taken over the Phereless! The Skyguard cannot control this draconian onslaught by ourselves. We desperately need assistance! Take out 15 of those reptilian ruffians before things get TOO out of hand!

- When Completed

You sure took a chunk out of the Sky Pirate army. But they just keep coming. We are going to have to take more drastic measures in case things make a turn for the worse…

  • Slain Sky Pirate x15

Rewards:

  • 400 Gold
  • 400 Exp

The Phereless is powered by a steam motor found beneath Her main deck. If the Sky Pirates end up taking over the ship entirely… the amount of damage they could cause to Lore is incalculable. We must cut off power to the ship in case they succeed!

Before the Phereless was hijacked, the Skyguard received a shipment of Gel-Oh-No brand gelatin treats (hey, it's a healthy treat and fun to eat!). If we could throw enough Gel-Oh-No into the ship's boiler… it just might clog it up. Search the entire ship for 10 of these boxes and toss them in the boiler!

+ When Completed
- When Completed

…it worked? I mean OF COURSE it worked! But… uhhh… did you just feel that jolt? Uh-oh….

  • Boiler Clogged x1

Rewards:

  • 550 Gold
  • 500 Exp

Thanks to Hina.

Requirements: Must have completed previous quest.

Something is wrong down in the engine room. The boiler is clogged… but *something* is moving around down there. You don't think… from all the Gel-O-No… no no no no… no way… but maybe. Hurry back to the engine room and find out what's going on down there!

+ When Completed
- When Completed

Items Required:

  • Gel-O-No Defeated x1
    • Dropped by Rehydrated Gell Oh No

Rewards:

  • 600 Gold
  • 650 Exp

This fight will not be easy. I will stand guard here while you take out that big boy. I wish you the best of luck.

+ When Completed
- When Completed

  • Sky Pirate Dragon Slain x1
    • Dropped by Sky Pirate Dragon

Rewards:

  • 800 Gold
  • 700 Exp

Thanks to Hina.

Requirements: Must have completed previous quest.

WE have the upper hand! Now is the time to fight with all our might! Gladius won't be too confident once the Skyguard have taken back our ship! Travel the Phereless once more and collect Medals of Dishonor from his minions that are slain by your hand. Hurry Hero—we CAN win this!

+ When Completed
- When Completed

Yes! You did it! Now only one more threat stands in the way of taking back the Phereless… Gladius.

  • Sky Pirate Medal of Dishonor x8

  • Gel-O-No Medal of Dishonor x1
    • Dropped by Rehydrated Gell Oh No
  • Sky Dragon Medal of Dishonor x1
    • Dropped by Sky Pirate Dragon

  • 850 Gold
  • 800 Exp

Thanks to Hina.

Requirements: Must have completed previous quest.

This is it! The Sky Pirates are on the run and only one monster remains: Gladius. Not much is known about this fiend other than he (or she?) is the leader of the Sky Pirates. Not even the Skyguard can take him down. It is up to you brave hero! Arm yourself for battle!

+ When Completed
- When Completed

YES You did it! The Phereless belongs to the Skyguard once more! You will forever be honored by King Alteon and the Skyguard! Thank you, brave hero!

  • Gladius Defeated x1

  • 1,500 Exp

We've got bags of sand lying around to mop up oil spills and I'll also need SteamOcto Tentacles to provide suction to hold the glass in, so bring me 7 of those.

- When Completed

  • Chaobold Bullies

School's out for the invasion! School's out forever, because school's been blown to pieces! Out for summer, out for fall, the students might not go back at all, if we don't take down these invaders. Scout out the locations of all monsters and report back to me, Hero.

+ When Completed
- When Completed

Alright, we know what we're facing. This is an education of higher learning. WAY higher. So lets hope these cretinous chaos creatures underestimate what we can do.

  • Academy Explored x1
    • Explore the map

Rewards:

  • 200 Gold
  • 200 Exp
  • 250 Rep : Skyguard

Thanks to Tendou no Mazo and XPoint.

Requirements: Must have completed previous quest.

The invader-instigator has flooded our fair halls with these chaos-tainted creatures, but this is the first I've heard of a new wave of mayhem-minded monsters stirring. Where are they coming from and… why? Interrogate 9 Chaobolds, 2 Sky Pirates, and collect 5 SkyGuard Academy history books to find out!

+ When Completed
- When Completed

Unfortunately, the History books were no help in understanding their Master's motive. And those Chaobolds are tricksy creatures. I don't know whether to believe what they've told you. Sent to a new master by their older one? Tales of raging chaos to come? That last one did drop an interesting hint, though, didn't it? *For Master it… personal. For us, is only chaos-mayhem-madness: Life!*

Items Required:

  • Chaobold Interrogated x9
    • Dropped by Chaobold
  • Academy History Book x 5
    • Collect the books around the location
  • Pirate Interrogated x2
    • Dropped by Bronze Sky Pirate

Rewards:

  • 250 Gold
  • 250 Exp
  • 300 Rep : Skyguard

Requirements: Must have completed previous quest.

The enemies just keep coming! Even with the help of the new recruits, we're not able to hold them. The Academy is crossed by semi-automatic tripwires which will take the Chaobolds down. We just need to activate the sensors! There are 15 different tripewire activation spots hidden around the school, so look carefully.

+ When Completed
- When Completed

Well done, Hero! Just look, the Chaobolds numbers are thinning already! Now that we have more even numbers, even the untrained recruits will be able to mop up the remaining monsters.

  • Tripwire Sensor Activated x15
    • Activate the sensors around the place

Rewards:

  • 300 Gold
  • 300 Exp
  • 350 Rep : Skyguard

I've seen these before. Imbunche guard the lairs of magicians-warlocks, wizards, witches, and the like. If one of these has been sent away from its post, it can only mean its Master is on the move. I don't know if a telephatic link between Master and minions exists, so to be on the safe side, render it unable to report!

  • Imbunche Impeded x1
