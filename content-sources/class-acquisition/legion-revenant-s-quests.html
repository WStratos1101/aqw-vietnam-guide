Legion Revenant's Quests
AQWorlds Wiki » World » Quests » Legion Revenant's Quests

Quest Location: Revenant (Location)
Quests Begun From: Legion Revenant (NPC)

  • Legion Fealty 1
  • Legion Fealty 2
  • Legion Fealty 3
  • Legion Fealty 4

Requirements: Requires Rank 10 Evil.

Every member of the Legion is undead to some degree, either through losing their physical life or forfeiting their souls. As Dage’s most loyal servants, Legion Revenants bask in death and dutifully spread unlife in the name of the Legion. Kill and re-kill Ultra Aeacus 50 times to further his strength in death, tether 300 forgotten souls to the Undead Legion, darken the essence of 500 Shadow Warriors in /shadowrealmpast, and return with contracts from 1,000 dracolich heads. The cost is high, but your reward will be great.

+ When Completed
- When Completed

You have done well, but this alone will not suffice. Prove your dedication and continue strengthening the Legion through death with your actions. Countless members of the legion desire unfathomable power, but few will earn it-- such a high cost is required for such great strength. Are you among the worthy?

Items Required:

  • Aeacus Empowered x50
  • Tethered Soul x300
  • Darkened Essence x500
  • Dracolich Contract x1,000

Rewards:

  • 50,000 Gold
  • 50,000 Exp

Items:

  • Revenant's Spellscroll

Thanks to Taryth and Tris.

Requirements:

  • Must have completed the previous quest.
  • Must be Level 80.

The Legion is vast, and our influence is among the strongest in Lore. However, the existence of other undead factions means that our control over unlife is not yet ubiquitous. Prove your loyalty to the Legion by slaying one legion's worth of undead not yet pledged to Dage— perhaps in their next unlife, they will bend to our will. The cost is high, but your reward will be great.

+ When Completed
- When Completed

You have single handedly defeated an army of undead in the Legion's name, but you will need to defeat more than just one army to earn the complete command over death you seek. Those whose ranks you wish to join have completed this same task countless times. I will save my praise for when you stand among them-- if you ever will.

Items Required:

  • Grim Cohort Conquered x400
  • Ancient Cohort Conquered x400
  • Pirate Cohort Conquered x400
  • Battleon Cohort Conquered x400
  • Mirror Cohort Conquered x400
  • Darkblood Cohort Conquered x400
  • Vampire Cohort Conquered x400
  • Spirit Cohort Conquered x400
  • Dragon Cohort Conquered x400
  • Doomwood Cohort Conquered x400

Rewards:

  • 50,000 Gold
  • 50,000 Exp

Items:

  • Conquest Wreath

Thanks to Taryth and Tris.

Requirements: Must have completed the previous quest.

You have shown your loyalty through tasks expected from Legion Revenants, but have yet to stand out among other members of the Undead Legion. If you want to join the Legion's most elite, then you must show your willingness to work harder than every other soldier. This is the price you must pay before you can accept you final task. The cost is high, but your reward will be great.

+ When Completed
- When Completed

Your dedication to the Legion is second to none, but this display of loyalty is not the last you will need to demonstrate before receiving one of the greatest gifts Dage can bestow. You have come far, have you realized it is too late to turn back?

Items Required:

  • Hooded Legion Cowl x1
  • Legion Token x4,000
  • Dage's Favor x300
  • Emblem of Dage x1
  • Diamond Token of Dage x30
  • Dark Token x100

Rewards:

  • 50,000 Gold
  • 50,000 Exp

Items:

  • Exalted Crown

Thanks to Taryth and Tris.

Requirements: Must have completed the previous quest.

Your previous displays of loyalty were impressive, but not enough. For your final task, you must prove that you are worthy to command the power granted to Legion Revenants. For all the death you will spread, all the legions you will conquer, all the currency you will donate, the Legion will grow stronger. A task fit for an army assigned to but a single soldier-- should you complete it, you will be deemed worthy of the strength you seek.

+ When Completed
- When Completed

You have been deemed worthy to command the ultimate power of the Legion. Such terrible strength may only be granted to those with the undying will to earn it. Could there be a darkness stronger than that which you will receive?

Items Required:

  • Exalted Crown x10
  • Revenant's Spellscroll x20
  • Conquest Wreath x6

Rewards:

  • 50,000 Gold
  • 50,000 Exp
  • Legion Revenant Character Page Badge

Items:

  • Exalted Crown
  • Legion Revenant (Class)
  • Legion Revenant Armor

Thanks to Taryth and Tris.

Begin this quest in our free web game at www.AQ.com!
