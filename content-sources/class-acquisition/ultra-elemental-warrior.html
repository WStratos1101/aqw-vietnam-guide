Ultra Elemental Warrior
AQWorlds Wiki » Items » Classes » Ultra Elemental Warrior

Location: Ultra Elemental Class - Curiosities
Price: N/A

  • Merge the following:
    • Darkness Orb x1
    • Earth Orb x1
    • Energy Orb x1
    • Fire Orb (Cape) x1
    • Ice Orb (Cape) x1
    • Light Orb x1
    • Water Orb x1
    • Wind Orb x1

Sellback: 0 AC
Weapon Damage: 80%, 2.0 speed
Description: Recommended enhancement: Hybrid. You've wielded the 8 Elementals and are now able to MASTER them as an Ultra Elemental Warrior!
Stat Model: Full Hybrid
Special Effects:

  • Elemental Warriors gain mana when they:
    • Strike an enemy in combat (more effective on crits)
    • Are struck by an enemy in combat

Skills:

+ Elemental Axe
- Elemental Axe

Elemental Axe
Rank Needed: 0
Mana Cost: 0 Mana
Cooldown: 2 Seconds
Type: Physical
Range: A medium range attack that deals damage to 1 target.
Description: Strike your opponents from a further range with your Axe.

+ Elemental Invocation
- Elemental Invocation

Elemental Invocation
Rank Needed: 1
Mana Cost: 6 Mana
Cooldown: 4 Seconds
Type: Hybrid
Range: A medium range attack that deals damage to 1 target.
Description: Infuse yourself with Elemental Power, reducing your mana cost and Damage Taken by 15% for 5 seconds and priming your elemental abilities. Your blade rends the air, inflicting your foe with a Mana Rift, decreasing their Dodge and Crit Chance by 15% for 10 seconds. Cannot miss but cannot crit.

+ Blaze
- Blaze

Blaze
Rank Needed: 2
Mana Cost: 10 Mana
Cooldown: 4 Seconds
Type: Hybrid
Range: A long attack that deals damage to 1 target.
Description: Strike your opponent with fiery wrath, dealing large damage and converting your Elemental Power to Fire Magic. If you possess Earth Magic, the fusion makes you Incandescent, increasing your Endurance by 100% and Healing Intake by 200% for 20 seconds. If you possess Energy Magic, the fusion grants you Critical Mass, increasing your Crit Chance by 25% and Crit Damage by 125% for 10 seconds.

+ Stoneskin
- Stoneskin

Stoneskin
Rank Needed: 3
Mana Cost: 12 Mana
Cooldown: 4 Seconds
Type: Hybrid
Range: A short range skill that heals yourself.
Description: Call upon the power of the earth, healing yourself. If Elemental Power is present, you heal twice as much and convert it into Earth magic. If you possess Fire Magic, the fusion creates Molten Armor, reducing the damage you take by 60% for 20 seconds. If you possess Energy Magic, the fusion grants you an Earthen Blessing, healing you for a small amount over the next 20 seconds.

+ Command of Darkness
- Command of Darkness

Command of Darkness
Rank Needed: 4
Type: Passive Ability
Description: The power of darkness grants you 15% increased Strength and 75% increased Endurance.

+ Command of Light
- Command of Light

Command of Light
Rank Needed: 4
Type: Passive Ability
Description: The power of Light grants you 15% increased Intellect and 12% increased Haste and Hit Chance.

+ Voltaic Overload
- Voltaic Overload

Voltaic Overload
Rank Needed: 5
Mana Cost: 12 Mana
Cooldown: 6 Seconds
Type: Hybrid
Range: A long attack that deals damage to 1 target.
Description: Overloaded mana bursts forth from your weapon, dealing great damage to your foe based on your current mana and converting your Elemental Power to Energy Magic. If you possess Fire Magic, the fusion turns you into a Superconductor, increasing your Damage by 50% for 10 seconds. If you possess Earth Magic, the fusion Grounds you, increasing your Haste by 20% for 10 seconds.

+ Blizzard
- Blizzard

Blizzard
Rank Needed: 10
Type: Passive Ability
Description: Starting at rank 1, when invoking Elemental Power, you have a very small chance of creating powerful Ice Magic. Fusing this magic with different elements can lead to very brief but powerful effects.

Ice and Fire makes you Supercritical, increasing your STR and INT by 200%, and LUK by 20%.
Ice and Earth creates Hailstone, healing you greatly.
Ice and Energy creates Hoarfrost, reducing your Mana Cost by 200% for 5 seconds- causing you to generate mana instead!

Notes:

  • Required to access the Ultra Elemental Gear shop.
  • This class has the same skills as:
    • Legendary Elemental Warrior.
    • Mythic Elemental Warrior (Class).
  • Also see:
    • Ultra Elemental Warrior Armor (Legend).
    • Ultra Elemental Warrior Armor (Non-Legend).
