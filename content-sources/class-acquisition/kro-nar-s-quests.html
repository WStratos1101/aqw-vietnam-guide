If you are to tap into a portion of the Eternal Dragon of Time's power, you must first know more about its strength.

Battle Mummies in /cruxship or /mummies to piece together information from the Lost Hieroglyphics they hold.

Recover Historia Pages from enemies in /timelibrary.

Seek out a copy of Myths of Lore from Maya's Shop in /librarium.

Defeat the Frost King in /kingcoal to earn the information he holds.

Finally, rediscover what you already know; seek an answer from yourself in /baconcatyou.

Now that you've recovered more knowledge of the Eternal Dragon of Time, you should learn more about the powers that all dragons possess. Cross swords with Desoloth in /dragonchallenge, Goregold in /greed, Victorious in /darkplane, and the adult Trigoras in /trigoras to understand the ferocity you possess. You'll need plenty of patience if you're to continue this journey too— I hear Nythera in /blindingsnow knows a thing or two about that, but good luck finding her!

Attune yourself to the currents of time when you win Timestream String from Timestream Riders in /arena.

Finally, travel through time and bring back Stellaria's Pink Blade of Destruction in /techfortress.
