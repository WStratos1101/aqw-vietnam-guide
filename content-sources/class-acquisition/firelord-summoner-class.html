FireLord Summoner (Class)
AQWorlds Wiki » Items » Classes » FireLord Summoner (Class)

Location: FireLord Summoner (Shop) - Book of Lore
Price: 0 AC
Sellback: 0 AC
Weapon Damage: 100%, 2.0 speed
Description: Recommended enhancement: Wizard. FireLord Summoners control the power of fire and darkness. They are known to burn their enemies to a crisp and bind their souls into service.
Stat Model: Offensive Caster
Special Effects: Blaze Binders gain mana from all hits landed in combat, and especially on crits. The amount depends on the damage relative to *their own* HP total.
Skills:

+ Fire Bolt
- Fire Bolt

Fire Bolt
Rank Needed: 0
Mana Cost: 0 Mana
Cooldown: 2 Seconds
Type: Physical
Range: A long range attack that deals damage to 3 hostile targets.
Description: A basic attack, taught to all adventurers. Damage dealt is based on your weapon damage. Blast your opponents at long range with force bolts.
Notes:

  • Previously called "Force Bolt" and before "Auto Attack".
  • Old icon.
+ Combustion Blaze
- Combustion Blaze

Combustion Blaze
Rank Needed: 1
Mana Cost: 10 Mana
Cooldown: 4 Seconds
Type: Magical
Range: A long range attack that deals damage to 3 hostile targets.
Description: Unleash explosive flames on three enemies and then watch them burn with a small DoT for 4 seconds afterwards.
Notes:

  • This skill is guaranteed to crit.
  • This skill is unavoidable.
  • Applies Combustion Blaze to 3 enemies, dealing a DoT over 4 seconds.
+ Choking Shadow Smoke
- Choking Shadow Smoke

Choking Shadow Smoke
Rank Needed: 2
Mana Cost: 25 Mana
Cooldown: 8 Seconds
Type: Magical
Range: A long range status skill that applies to 3 hostile targets.
Description: Your smoke is infused with the power of Darkness and causes your opponents to have a reduced chance of hitting you and occasionally stuns them for 3 seconds. Inhaling the smoke also causes a large DoT lasting for 8 seconds.
Notes:

  • This skill is unavoidable but does not deal damage on hit.
  • Applies Shadow Smoked to 3 targets, causing a DoT over 8 seconds and reducing hit chance by 30%.
  • Has a chance to apply choked to enemy targets, stunning them for 4 seconds.
+ Fire in Your Skin
- Fire in Your Skin

Fire in Your Skin
Rank Needed: 3
Mana Cost: 30 Mana
Cooldown: 16 Seconds
Type: Magical
Range: A short range status skill that applies to yourself.
Description: Kindle a flame within you, setting yourself ablaze. Applies Burning Ward which reduces damage taken by 40% while damaging opponents that attack you for 6 seconds, and Blazing Speed which increases haste by 15% for 14 seconds. You also use your darkness powers to restore your life from the ashes around you to apply an HoT for 8 seconds.
Notes:

  • The name of this skill is a direct reference to the Disney song Hellfire.
  • Applies Burning Ward to caster for 6 seconds, increasing caster's damage resistance by 40% and dealing damage to enemies that attack you.
  • Applies Fire in Your Skin to caster for 8 seconds, applying a HoT.
  • Applies Blazing Speed to caster for 14 seconds, Increasing haste by 15%.

+ Dark Fire
- Dark Fire

Dark Fire
Rank Needed: 5
Mana Cost: 25 Mana
Cooldown: 12 Seconds
Type: Magical
Range: A long range attack that deals damage to 3 hostile targets.
Description: You unleash a devastating burst of Dark Fire on 3 opponents. They all take heavy damage and continue to burn from a reasonable DoT for 8 seconds. The fire will also apply either a 10% DoT damage taken debuff or a -10% damage debuff to your enemy.
Notes:

  • Deals 350% spell damage on impact.
  • This skill is guaranteed to non-crit.
  • This skill is unavoidable.
  • Applies Dark Fire to target enemies, dealing DoT over 8 seconds.
  • Has a chance to apply Burnt to a Crisp for 10 seconds, increasing DoT damage by 10%.
  • Has a chance to apply Shrouded in Shadow Flame for 10 seconds, decreasing enemy damage by 10%.
