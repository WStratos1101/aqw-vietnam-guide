Chrono Assassin (Merge)
AQWorlds Wiki » Items » Classes » Chrono Assassin (Merge)

Location: Tachyon Merge - Deep Portalspace
Price: N/A
  • Merge the following:
    • Saeculum Gem x12

Sellback: 0 AC
Weapon Damage: 50%, 1.0 speed
Description: Recommended enhancement: Thief. Master time and space like Xeven. Tear through the fabric of time literally as a Chrono Assassin.
Stat Model: Dodge Melee
Special Effects:

  • Time Assassins gain mana when they:
    • Strike an enemy in combat (more effective on crits)
    • Dodge any attack (restores HP as well)

Skills:

+ Strike Blades
- Strike Blades

Strike Blades
Rank Needed: 0
Mana Cost: 0 Mana
Cooldown: 1 Second
Type: Physical
Range: A short range attack that deals damage to 1 target.
Description: You wield Strike Blades. Manipulating with time allows you to attack incredibly fast, but sacrificing power.

+ Shift
- Shift

Shift
Rank Needed: 1
Mana Cost: 12 Mana
Cooldown: 5 Seconds
Type: Physical
Range: A long range attack that deals damage to 1 target.
Description: Push time forward with your next strike dealing Light Damage to your opponent. Applies Push Time, increasing your Dodge and Haste by 8% for 12 seconds, stacks to 5. If Reverse Time is in play, Strike Blades will replace it with Temporal Blades increasing your Damage Output by 30% and applying Pull Time, lowering the opponent's Haste and Dodge by 15% for 12 seconds.
Notes:
*If Reverse Time is active, it is replaced with Overse Time for 1 second.

  • Temporal Blades is not refreshed when re-applied.
+ Relapse
- Relapse

Relapse
Rank Needed: 2
Mana Cost: 6 Mana
Cooldown: 3 Seconds
Type: Hybrid
Range: An infinite range attack that deals damage to 1 target.
Description: Relieve time with your next strike dealing Moderate Damage to your opponent. Applies Deadlock to yourself, stacks 4 times. Once max stacked Deadlock will consume itself and apply Reverse Time, granting you a Moderate HoT for 10 seconds.
Notes:

  • Applies Deadlock to caster for 12 seconds.
  • At 5 stacks of Deadlock, 4 are consumed to apply Reverse Time to caster for 10 seconds.
+ Convert
- Convert

Convert
Rank Needed: 3
Mana Cost: 12 Mana
Cooldown: 4 Seconds
Type: Physical
Range: A long range attack that deals damage to 1 target.
Description: Generate time with your next strike dealing Moderate Damage to your opponent. Applies Condense Time, increasing Critical Strike Chance and Critical Strike Damage by 10% for 12 seconds, stacks to 5. If Reverse Time is in play, Strike Blades will replace it with Temporal Blades increasing all damage out by 30% and apply Depress Time, lowering the opponent's Critical Strike Chance and Critical Strike Damage by 15% for 12 seconds.
Notes:

  • If Reverse Time is active, it is replaced with Overse Time for 1 second.
  • Temporal Blades is not refreshed when re-applied.

- Anticipation

Anticipation

+ Hysteria
- Hysteria

Hysteria
Rank Needed: 5
Mana Cost: 30 Mana
Cooldown: 20 Seconds
Type: Magical
Range: An infinite range status skill that applies to 1 target.
Description: Sever your foe's mind in time causing them to go insane. For the next 6 seconds the target will hit itself instead of you. If Reverse Time isn't in play you get Enhance Time, reducing the damage your target takes by 15%. If Reverse Time is in play, its removed and you get Temporal Blades while your opponent gets Corrupt Time. Temporal Blades increases your damage by 30%. Corrupt Time increases your opponent's damage by 15%. They last 12 seconds.
Notes:

  • If Reverse Time is active, it is replaced with Overse Time for 1 second.
  • Temporal Blades is not refreshed when re-applied.

Notes:

  • Class breakdown Design Notes post.
  • This class has the same skills as Chrono Assassin (AC).
  • Also see:
    • Chrono Assassin Armor (AC).
    • Chrono Assassin Armor (Merge).
    • Xeven.

  • Female
  • Old

Note: Male and Female look the same.
