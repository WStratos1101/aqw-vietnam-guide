Overworld Chronomancer (Merge)
AQWorlds Wiki » Items » Classes » Overworld Chronomancer (Merge)

Location: HeroPoints Merge - HeroMart
Price: N/A

  • Merge the following:
    • HeroPoint x5

Sellback: 0 AC
Weapon Damage: 100%, 2.0 speed
Description: Recommended Enhancement: Lucky, Hybrid. Time holds no meaning deep in the underworld… Unless you are a Chronomancer! Overworld Chronomancers rely on Hourglasses and Timepieces they create using their Hourglass Pet (equippable items found in shop via the pet) to manipulate and distort time itself. (This class is a variant of Underworld Chronomancer)
Stat Model: Full Hybrid
Special Effects: Underworld Chronomancer gain mana from all hits landed in combat, and especially on critical hits. The amount depends on damage relative to *their own* HP total.
Skills:

+ Timeless Strike
- Timeless Strike

Timeless Strike
Rank Needed: 0
Mana Cost: 0 Mana
Cooldown: 2 Seconds
Type: Physical
Range: A short range attack that deals damage to 1 target.
Description: Deals damage based on weapon damage. Each of your skills gets an additional benefit from hourglasses you equip in your consumables slot. Hourglasses can be obtained from the hourglass pet available from the Book of Lore.
Notes:

  • If Timepiece of Madness is active, applies the following effects:
  • Applies one stack of Mad for every use of Timeless Strike.
  • At 5 stacks of Mad, the next Timeless Strike consumes these stacks and applies Eternal.
  • Eternal boosts your critical strike chance by 80% and reduces the damage you deal by 30%.
+ Timeless Rift
- Timeless Rift

Timeless Rift
Rank Needed: 1
Mana Cost: 15 Mana
Cooldown: 2.5 Seconds
Type: Hybrid
Range: A short range attack that deals damage to 1 target.
Description: Deals damage to your target and applies Temporal Rift to you. Temporal Rift stacks 4 times, lasts 30 seconds and is used by your other abilities.

+ Hourglass Reversal
- Hourglass Reversal

Hourglass Reversal
Rank Needed: 2
Mana Cost: 30 Mana
Cooldown: 8 Seconds
Type: Hybrid
Range: An infinite range skill that heals yourself.
Description: Reverses time to heal you. Heal is increased by stacks of Temporal Rift and consumes those stacks.
Notes:

  • If Hourglass of Power is active, applies Power for 30 seconds, increasing damage, haste, crit chance, crit damage, hit chance, dodge chance and damage reduction by 10%.
  • If Infinite Corruption is active, applies Hourglass Heal for 20 seconds, a Heal over Time.
+ Distortion through Time
- Distortion through Time

Distortion through Time
Rank Needed: 3
Mana Cost: 20 Mana
Cooldown: 6 Seconds
Type: Hybrid
Range: A long range status skill that applies to 1 target.
Description: Applies Corruption Through Time. Corruption Through Time increases all damage you do by 20% for 10 seconds. Doesn’t stack.
Notes:

  • If Entropic Corruption is active, applies the following effects:
  • Entropic Harm for 10 seconds, increasing damage taken by 150%.
  • Entropic Mana for 7 seconds, increasing haste by 30% and reducing mana costs by 50%.
  • Entropic Power for 4 seconds, increasing damage by 100%.
  • Focus for 3 seconds, forcing affected monsters to attack you.
  • If Foresee Corruption is active, applies the following effects:
  • Foresee for 150 seconds.
  • Using Foresee Corruption again replaces this effect with Seer for 20 seconds.
  • Using Corruption Through Time again replaces Seer with Change the Future.
  • Change the Future applies Focus to enemies, and decreases damage taken by 80%, and increases dodge chance, haste, hit chance, damage, and crit damage by 100% for 5 seconds.
  • If Hourglass of Paradise is active, applies the following effects (or removes them if they are currently active):
  • Welcome to Paradise, reducing damage taken by 40% and increasing haste by 30%. In addition, provides a Heal over Time that also regenerates mana.
  • Price of Paradise, reducing crit chance and crit damage by 40%.
  • If Hourglass of Transience is active, applies the following effects:
  • Transient for 10 seconds, a self DoT that increases in strength.
  • Using Corruption Through Time again replaces Transient with Ephemeral for 5 seconds, increasing dodge and damage by +50%.
  • If Timepiece of Madness is active, applies the following effects:
  • Applies Potential, increasing your Hit Chance by 20% for 20 seconds. Cooldown: 12 seconds.
  • If Timepiece of Inevitability is active, applies the following effects:
  • Applies Inevitable Corruption, increasing your outgoing damage and haste by 12% for 20 seconds.
  • Using the Timepiece of Inevitability while Inevitable Corruption is active applies Snap to your opponent, dealing significant damage to them after a short delay. Cooldown: 20 seconds.
  • If Timepiece of Pestilence is active, applies the following effects:
  • Applies Infectious Air, increasing the amount of damage the enemy takes by 20% for 10 seconds, stacking to 3 but is consumed by Temporal Collapse.
  • Your incoming damage is increased by 100% for 10 seconds.
  • Applies a light DoT to your opponent.
  • Applies Decay to your opponent, preventing them from healing.
